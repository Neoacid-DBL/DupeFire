WhatsApp filling your phone? What to delete without losing what matters

Your phone warns you there's no space and WhatsApp is among the biggest items. That's normal: it saves every photo, video, voice note and document you receive. The good news is that almost all of it can be cleaned without touching your messages.

Updated October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer
  1. In WhatsApp open Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage and start with "Forwarded many times" and "Larger than 5 MB".
  2. Then go into the chats and groups that weigh the most and delete the videos, which are usually the biggest part.
  3. For repeated copies and near-identical photos, which WhatsApp can't detect, use an app like DupeFire (the Chats tool). And turn off auto-download so it doesn't fill up again.

What takes up so much space in WhatsApp

It isn't text messages, which weigh almost nothing. It's the media files:

  • Videos: by far the heaviest, especially from very active groups.
  • Photos and memes: light one by one, but repeated across chats.
  • Voice notes and audio, which add up over the years.
  • Documents: PDFs, spreadsheets and forwarded files.
  • Stickers and GIFs.

WhatsApp stores everything in Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/ (for WhatsApp Business, com.whatsapp.w4b). Look at that folder with the Files app to see how much each type weighs.

Step 1: use WhatsApp's storage manager

  1. In WhatsApp, open Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage.
  2. Open "Forwarded many times": chain messages and memes. Select all and delete.
  3. Open "Larger than 5 MB": mostly videos. Review and delete what you don't want.
  4. Further down, chats are sorted by size. Open the heaviest ones and delete in batches, sorting by size.
⚠️

What you delete here disappears from your phone and from the chat. If a photo or video matters, save it to your gallery or somewhere else first.

Step 2: remove the repeated copies

This is the space WhatsApp can't see: the same meme saved five times, the photo three people sent you, the copy of what you send in the Sent folder. With DupeFire:

  1. Install DupeFire from Google Play and grant access to your photos and videos.

  3. Review with the preview and choose what to keep.
  4. Confirm. Files go to the system trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Step 3: stop it filling up again

  • Auto-download. In Settings → Storage and data → Media auto-download, set "No media" for mobile data, Wi-Fi and roaming, or photos only. You'll only download what you open.
  • Media visibility. In Settings → Chats → Media visibility, turn it off if you don't want WhatsApp stuff filling your gallery.
  • Very active groups. Mute the ones that generate the most media or turn on disappearing messages; files already downloaded stay on the phone, so clean them now and then.
  • Monthly check. Five minutes a month beats an emergency clean-up when nothing fits anymore.

If you also use Telegram

Telegram keeps a cache you can empty without losing messages, because files can be downloaded again from its servers: Settings → Data and storage → Storage usage → Clear cache (names may vary by version). If "Save to gallery" is on, photos are also repeated in Pictures/Telegram.

FAQ

If I delete files from my phone, are they deleted for the other person?

No. What you delete on your phone only affects your copy; the other person keeps theirs.

Do I lose text messages when I delete media?

No. Messages stay in the chat; what disappears are the photos, videos or documents you delete, which can no longer be opened.

What about my WhatsApp backup?

The Google Drive backup is rebuilt from what's on your phone, so the next backup won't include the deleted files. If something matters, save it before cleaning.
