Best duplicate cleaner apps for Android: an honest comparison

There are dozens of "cleaner" apps on Google Play and many aren't worth it. Here we go through the real options, when each makes sense and where DupeFire is better or worse. Disclosure: DupeFire is our app, so we say that up front and are just as clear about its limits.

What to check before installing a duplicate cleaner

  • What it detects. Only exact copies, or visually similar photos too?
  • How it deletes. Ideally with a preview and confirmation, and with files going to the system trash so you can recover them.
  • Privacy. Does it analyze on the phone or upload your photos to a server? Read the Play listing and the privacy policy.
  • Permissions and ads. Be wary of apps that ask for permissions they don't need or block the main function behind ads.
  • Recent reviews. Read those from the last few months, not just the average rating.

The options, one by one

Files by Google

Free and from Google. Its Clean tab usually suggests duplicate files, large files and junk. It's a good first pass for identical copies. What it doesn't do is group near-identical photos (recompressed, bursts, crops), and its options depend on the version.

Manufacturer cleaner (Samsung, Xiaomi, etc.)

Already installed and trustworthy. What it offers varies a lot by brand and model; some suggest duplicates and large files. If it doesn't offer the feature you want, you can't add it.

Google Photos

It isn't a duplicate finder but a backup and organizer. Its "Free up space" feature removes from the phone what's already uploaded, which is a different job.

SD Maid (SE)

A powerful tool for people with some technical knowledge: it cleans app leftovers and cache and can look for duplicates. It's more technical and may intimidate someone who just wants to delete a few photos; some features may be paid, so check the current listing.

FAQ

Is there a duplicate app that's totally free and ad-free?

System tools (like Files by Google or the manufacturer's cleaner) are usually free. Among independent apps, almost all are supported by ads or a paid version; check the current Google Play listing.

Is it safe to let an app see my photos?

It's necessary for it to analyze them. Install only apps from developers you trust, read their privacy policy and check whether analysis runs on the phone.

Can I recover what I delete by mistake?

It depends on the app. DupeFire sends files to the system trash, where you can restore them for 30 days. Ask the same of any other.
